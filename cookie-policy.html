Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Tech Group Kenya (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on techgroupkenya.co.ke.

Do we use cookies?

No. We do not use cookies on this website. We do not place cookies for advertising, profiling, retargeting, or selling personal data.

What about analytics?

We use privacy-first analytics (Simple Analytics) that does not use cookies and respects Do Not Track. It collects aggregate, non-identifying visit statistics only.

Browser storage (not cookies)

Our contact form may use temporary session storage in your browser solely to limit spam submissions. Session storage is not a cookie, stays on your device, and is cleared when you close the browser tab or window.

Linked product sites

When you leave this site for our products—Tech Hub, Tech Events, Tech Blog, or the Community Hub—those properties may use their own technologies under their own policies. This Cookie Policy applies only to techgroupkenya.co.ke.

Your choices

Because we do not set cookies on this site, there is no cookie consent banner to manage. You can still control cookies for other sites in your browser settings.

Related policies

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for how we handle personal information and use of the Services.
